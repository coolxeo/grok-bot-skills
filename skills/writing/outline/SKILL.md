# Outline

Turn a seed, interview, or signed facts into a writing plan. Do not write the article yet.

## Arc

1. **Opening hook** — 2–3 specific options from lived detail, not “open with an anecdote.”
2. **Problem / question** — the tension. Why the reader should care.
3. **Mechanism** — the system or pattern that explains it.
4. **Evidence** — named facts, files, measurements. Flag gaps.
5. **Implications** — what the reader can actually do.
6. **Close** — how to return to the opening. No fake-profound kicker.

## Rules

- Teach test: name the one thing the reader will learn.
- Vary section length. Do not make every block the same shape.
- Present the outline for sign-off before drafting.
- Invent nothing. If a number, quote, or source is missing, mark the gap.
- After the outline is signed, draft section by section. Then run source-check, then No AI slop.
