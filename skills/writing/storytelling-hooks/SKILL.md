# Storytelling hooks

Write a hook from a fact you already have, or build a swipe-file carousel people can save and reuse. Do not paste another writer's lines.

This skill has two jobs. Use the one you were asked for.

1. Write one hook for a post you are about to publish.
2. Build a save-first swipe-file carousel of hook templates.

If you keep a private log of hooks that won or lost on an account, write three options here first, then check that log. This skill does not score past posts.

## Facts first

- Every blank is a fact you can name: a date, a cost, a quoted question, a rule you actually follow.
- If you do not have the fact, pick another family. Do not invent a number, a client, or a year.
- The rest of the post must pay off the hook. If the hook promises one rule, the post has one rule. If it names a cost, the post gives that cost.

## Families

Full templates live in [references/patterns.md](references/patterns.md). Use the short table here to pick a family.

| Family | Use when | Why it works | One template |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transformation | You can name your own before and after | A gap between two states makes the reader want the path | `[Old state] in [month]. [New state] now. The change was smaller than you think.` |
| Mistake | You can name a failure and what it cost | A concrete cost makes the lesson specific | `This cost me [real cost]. Here is the step I skipped.` |
| Turning point | One event changed how you work | One cause is easy to remember | `I was about to drop [goal]. Then [one event].` |
| Behind the scenes | You can show the work behind a public result | People already saw the result and want the hidden effort | `[Result] took [real effort]. Here is the part nobody saw.` |
| Contrarian | You rejected common advice and have a record | The reader checks a belief they already hold | `Most people say [advice]. I tried the opposite for [period]. Here is what happened.` |
| Relatable | You can name a struggle the reader has now | Recognition comes before trust | `If [specific struggle] is your week, read this.` |
| Client or customer | A real client case taught you something | Proof comes from a third party, not from a claim | `A client asked [question]. The answer changed how we [do X].` |
| Curiosity | Something odd happened and you checked it | An unanswered question makes the reader stay | `[Thing] kept happening. The reason was not where I looked first.` |
| Lesson | You can pack experience into one rule | One line is easy to save | `[N] years of [work] left me with one rule: [rule].` |
| Decision | You chose between two named options | Two options create a real stake | `[Option A] or [Option B]. I picked [one]. Here is why.` |

## Write one hook

1. Name the fact the post can stand on, and the payoff the body can deliver.
2. Draft 3 hooks from 2 or 3 different families. Write them from the fact, not from a swipe file.
3. Drop any hook whose promise the body cannot keep. Drop any hook with an empty or invented blank.
4. Keep 1. Run [no-ai-slop](../no-ai-slop/) and [top-edit](../top-edit/) on that line.
5. Fit the line to the platform: on X it is the whole first line; on LinkedIn it must fit before "see more"; on a video it is the first spoken line.
6. For the rest of an X or LinkedIn post, hand off to [social-copywriting](../social-copywriting/).

Deliver the chosen hook in a code block. Under it, name the family, the fact that fills each blank, and the two hooks you did not use.

## Build a swipe-file carousel

Use this when the job is a save-first reference post, not a single hook. The shape below comes from the source table in [references/swipe-file-carousel-analysis.md](references/swipe-file-carousel-analysis.md) (read 18 Nov 2026, Europe/Madrid). Metrics in that file are a snapshot from public X API reads that day.

### Post text

One line: `[N] [noun] to [outcome]:`

The outcome is in the reader's words. The line ends in a colon, which points at the images. No link, no hashtag, no "bookmark this", no extra sentence.

`N` is groups times items, with the same item count on every content slide. A large round number reads as a library. The seed post used 10 groups of 8, which is 80.

### Slides

- **Slide 1 (cover).** The number is huge. Under it, the noun, then the outcome in a smaller line. A "Swipe" cue, a page counter, and a quiet footer with who made it.
- **Content slides.** One named group per slide. A one-line "use when" note. Then 6 to 8 numbered rows, each one fill-in sentence with the blanks marked. The row count does not change from slide to slide, so the grid stays still.
- **Last slide (optional).** Who made it, and where a longer version lives. Keep links off the post text itself.

In the 18 Nov 2026 table, the three multi-image posts had the three highest bookmarks per 1,000 impressions (42.8 on 9 slides, 38.1 on 6 slides, 34.4 on 11 slides). The sample is small. Treat that as a lead, not a rule.

### Visuals

Follow [DESIGN.md](../../../DESIGN.md). One light or dark token set, not a mix.

- Light: background `#f1f2ee` or surface `#fafaf7`, text `#14181b`, muted `#535b61`, rules `#d2d6ce`. If you use an accent, use blue `#2340c4` once, or spark `#F2762E` once and small.
- Dark: background `#111418` or surface `#171b20`, text `#e7e9e3`, muted `#9ea7ad`, rules `#2b3138`, blue `#a3b4ff`.
- Type: Newsreader for titles, Hanken Grotesk for the hook rows, DM Mono for labels, counters, and blanks. All three are SIL OFL.
- Format: 3:4 portrait (1080x1440 is enough). One grid on every slide. Hairlines from the rule token. No stock photos, no emoji, no lime `#C8FF3D`.

Use our templates from [references/patterns.md](references/patterns.md), not another writer's lines. Fill no blank in a published example unless the owner gave a real fact. For a demo carousel, leave the blanks visible.

Deliver a slide plan: the post text, the group name and "use when" line for each content slide, the 6 to 8 template rows, and the token set you will use. Do not invent engagement numbers for the plan.

## Never

- Copy a source hook word for word, or with only a light rewording.
- Invent a number, a client, or a year to fill a blank.
- Promise in the hook what the body does not deliver.
- Put a Gumroad link, a "bookmark this" line, or a link in the carousel post text.
