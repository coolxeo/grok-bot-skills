# Social copywriting

Write platform-native LinkedIn and X copy. Load after the idea exists. End with No AI slop.

## X

- Replies and bookmarks beat likes. External links in the body get throttled; put them in a reply.
- Single post: one idea, hook in the first line (first ~280 characters on long posts).
- Thread: 5–12 tweets. Tweet 1 is the hook only. Every tweet earns the next. Last tweet can ask or stop; no engagement bait.
- 0–2 hashtags. Skip them mid-thread.
- Do not paste LinkedIn copy onto X.
- Founder / build-in-public: real units (spend, views, files), not invented token bills.

## LinkedIn

- Hook before the “see more” fold (~140 characters on mobile).
- Body ~1,200–1,600 characters unless it is an article.
- No links in the body; “Link in comments.”
- 3–5 hashtags at the end. No engagement bait.
- LinkedIn copy stays with the LinkedIn writer unless asked otherwise.

## Hook discipline

If the opening is wrong, do not polish. Change the entry point. Weak hooks restate the topic or start too broad. Strong hooks name a specific pain, proof, image, or mechanism.

## Human voice (with No AI slop)

No delve/leverage/utilize/game-changer. No “it’s not X, it’s Y.” No “here’s the thing.” No em dashes in short copy. Vary sentence length. End without announcing the ending.

## Critique before rewrite

Name the job, the reader, the strongest existing line, what to keep, then rewrite.
